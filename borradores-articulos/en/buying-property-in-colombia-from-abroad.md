Thousands of Colombians abroad — and foreigners with ties to Colombia — are buying property back home, taking advantage of a favorable exchange rate. Here's what to know.

## You don't need to be in Colombia to buy

Much of the process can be done from abroad by granting power of attorney to someone you trust to sign on your behalf.

## Your rights are the same as any citizen's

Property is purchased by public deed (*escritura*) and registered in your name, with no restrictions for living overseas.

## Buying directly, without agents

Buying straight from the owner — with no agent commissions — is often clearer and cheaper. The key is proper due diligence: title study, tax clearances, and confirming land use before you sign.

## General steps

1. Identify the property and agree on terms with the owner.
2. Title study and legal verification (ideally with a lawyer).
3. Grant power of attorney to a trusted representative if you won't be present.
4. Sign the deed before a notary and register it.
5. Documented transfer of funds (keep every receipt).

## A note on trust

Be wary of pressure. A serious sale gives you time, documents, and answers in writing.
