Most articles about Colombia quote you Medellín prices. Bucaramanga is cheaper — often meaningfully so — but let's use real, honest ranges rather than a fantasy number.

## The short version

Estimates for a single person's monthly cost of living in Bucaramanga in 2026 range from roughly USD 575 to USD 965 *excluding rent*, depending heavily on lifestyle and which source you trust. A family of four typically lands somewhere around USD 1,100 to USD 2,000 excluding rent. Overall, living here tends to run at a fraction of a comparable North American or European city.

## Rent

A one-bedroom apartment in a central area averages somewhere in the USD 180–390/month range depending on the source and exact location; a three-bedroom runs higher. Desirable, established neighborhoods like Cabecera sit toward the upper, more comfortable end — you're paying for location, walkability, and services.

## Utilities

Basic utilities (electricity, water, garbage) for a mid-sized apartment average around USD 100/month. Internet and mobile are inexpensive by North American standards, typically under USD 20/month each.

## Food and transport

Groceries are cheap, especially if you shop the local markets (*plazas de mercado*) rather than imported goods. A local bus ride costs under USD 1.

## The honest caveat

Your real cost depends entirely on how you live: local markets vs. imported brands, one neighborhood vs. another, renting vs. owning. The numbers above are starting points, not promises.

## Why this matters if you're thinking of buying

If you own your home outright, your monthly cost of living drops dramatically — no rent, just utilities, food, and daily life. For many people that's the whole point of buying here: a low, predictable cost of living in a safe, green city.
