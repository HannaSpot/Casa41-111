Some houses arrive finished. This one arrives as a starting point — and for the right buyer, that's the appeal.

This is a multi-level home with generous rooms, interior patios, and character built over three decades. It isn't a flip. It's a canvas for someone who can see what a space could become. Below are honest directions to consider — with an important note at the end about what's permitted and what isn't.

## 1. A remodel-and-live family home

The most natural path. The bones, the light, and the room sizes are already here. A thoughtful renovation — modernizing kitchens and baths, opening key spaces, updating finishes — turns it into a spacious, character-rich home that newer construction simply can't replicate.

## 2. A multi-generational home

The different levels that make the house interesting also make it flexible. With the right design, separate levels can give privacy to extended family — grown children, visiting relatives, or aging parents — under one roof.

## 3. A home with a studio, workshop, or domestic-scale office

For a creative professional or someone who works from home, parts of the house lend themselves to a studio, atelier, or a small home office of domestic scale, integrated into a residential life.

## The honest part — please read this

You may look at this house and imagine a boutique hotel, short-stay rental, or a commercial space. It's a natural thought — the house has presence.

Here's the reality, stated plainly because you deserve it up front: the current zoning for this address is residential (the most protective category). Uses such as lodging, short-stay rental, formal offices, or commercial establishments are **not** currently permitted here as a matter of course. Any use beyond residential — and any structural modification — would need to be studied by a qualified architect and validated with the competent authorities *before* you count on it. We can't promise those uses, and we won't pretend otherwise.

What we *can* say honestly: this is a spacious, well-located home with room to be reimagined as a place to live. If your vision is a home with character that you shape into your own, this is a rare kind of opportunity.
