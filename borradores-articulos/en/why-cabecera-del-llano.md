When people ask where to live in Bucaramanga, the same answer keeps coming up: Cabecera. Here's why.

## It's established, not experimental

Cabecera (and the broader Altos de Cabecera) is a consolidated, mature part of the city — not an up-and-coming bet. Services, shops, restaurants, and parks are already here and already good.

## You can live without a car

This is rare and underrated. Cabecera is walkable: groceries, cafés, pharmacies, and green space are within reach on foot. For a lot of newcomers, that daily walkability is what makes a place feel like home.

## Parks and walking routes

Bucaramanga is the City of Parks, and Cabecera sits close to several — plus walking and hiking routes toward the eastern hills. It's normal to see neighbors out walking and exercising early in the morning.

## A real neighborhood rhythm

Cabecera feels lived-in and local, not like a tourist zone. That's precisely what people looking for an authentic daily life in Colombia are after.

## The trade-off, honestly

Being central and desirable, Cabecera isn't the absolute cheapest corner of the city — you pay a little more for location. Most residents consider it well worth it for the convenience and calm.
